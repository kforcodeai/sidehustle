# HighLevel Agency Operator Strategy From India

Start with a HighLevel implementation service for agencies already using the platform. Sell one defined project, demonstrate dependable delivery, and earn a repeat purchase. Test direct Indian business sales only when the export work is stable enough to support that experiment. Keep whichever route produces better contribution, manageable delivery and repeat business.

This is the operating decision guide for the launch kit. Use [Offers pricing and economics](02-offers-pricing-and-economics.md) for detailed commercial scope, [Client discovery and sales](03-client-discovery-and-sales.md) for scripts, and [Delivery and repeat business](04-delivery-and-repeat-business.md) for acceptance and support procedures. The broader India playbook is a source of ideas to validate, rather than a competing instruction to launch several services.

## Starting market and expansion conditions

Your automation skills fit implementation work. An agency already using HighLevel understands the platform, but still needs a current project, budget and reason to choose you. Begin with small UK agencies serving appointment-based businesses. Australia can be a later geographic test; do not add several countries and offers at once.

For the first 30 days, use two acquisition channels: individually researched agency outreach and closely matched Upwork applications. Use the same demonstration and sprint scope in both. Community participation and certification are optional supporting activities, not additional daily quotas.

Do not schedule an automatic move into India on day 45. Begin a small direct-business experiment when all these proposed operating conditions are met:

- Two paid implementation projects have been completed and accepted.
- At least one buyer has purchased again or renewed paid support.
- Actual delivery hours and direct costs show positive contribution at the price charged.
- Current work is on schedule, and at least six hours a week can be reserved for discovery for the next two weeks without compromising delivery.

An Indian agency buying the same sprint is a lower-complexity alternative channel. Test that route if the overseas offer has had a proper targeting and follow-up test without enough useful conversation, or a suitable warm opportunity appears. It does not require starting a direct-business managed service.

Do not shrink export work simply because five Indian clients have signed. Compare contribution per owner hour, acquisition effort, payment reliability, retention and concentration risk. Allocate capacity to the route that performs better. Direct Indian sales may remain a small experiment or become the main business; paid evidence should decide.

## The minimum offer

Sell the **HighLevel Lead to Booking Sprint**:

| Element | Included starting scope |
|---|---|
| Environment | One existing client sub-account, one business, one location and one language |
| Capture | One native form or one already functioning native lead source |
| Tracking | One pipeline with up to six stages and one agreed assignment process |
| Booking | One calendar with working hours, buffers and rescheduling rules |
| Workflow one | Capture or update the lead, assign ownership and notify the team |
| Workflow two | Up to three follow-up messages with reply, booking and opt-out stop conditions |
| Workflow three | Confirmation and up to two booking reminders, respecting cancellation and rescheduling |
| Channel | One eligible outbound channel; adapt client-supplied copy for up to six distinct outbound messages |
| Handover | Test results, workflow map, recorded walkthrough, one handover call and two revision rounds |

Aim for seven business days after the deposit, access, approved content and necessary channel approvals are complete. Internally budget up to 12 delivery hours, including testing and handover. Document missing dependencies and communicate a revised date when they delay work.

No-show recovery, review requests, additional sources, AI agents, custom integrations, migration and new advertising campaigns are separately scoped additions. Build the three-workflow version first. Create reusable configurations from working projects, with permission for any client-specific material, and retest every deployment.

### Starting agency prices

| Buyer | Pilot within the two-project launch allowance | Standard sprint | Monthly support |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Overseas agency | US$450 | US$750 | US$600 for up to eight delivery hours |
| Indian agency | ₹15,000 | ₹25,000 | ₹15,000 for up to eight delivery hours |

The two pilot places are shared across markets. Dollar prices mean US dollars. These are proposed prices to test, before applicable tax and payment-provider charges. The partner pays its HighLevel subscription and usage. Collect 50% before the project and the balance after the acceptance demonstration, before activation or final handover; follow marketplace milestone rules where applicable.

An optional **US$149 workflow diagnostic** can suit a buyer with a specific existing concern. Limit it to one journey containing up to three named workflows, with a maximum of two total work hours for access review, inspection and a short findings recording. It excludes repairs, a full account audit and a deliverability investigation. Quote a larger investigation separately. It is not a compulsory first purchase and may conclude that no paid follow-on work is needed.

After several similar implementations have been accepted, test demand for a reusable vertical configuration. US$1,500–2,500 is a possible quoting range to validate after defining included assets, deployment tests, documentation and support. It is not an initial promise or an assumed margin advantage.

### Support boundaries

The eight-hour plan covers up to three named sub-accounts. Scheduled checks, calls, documentation, fixes and minor changes all consume the allowance. There is one active request at a time. Warn the buyer at six hours and obtain approval before exceeding eight. New client launches remain separately priced sprints. Scoped correction of your original defects during the fourteen-day correction period does not consume paid support hours.

If proven demand requires more capacity, an overseas plan at US$1,200 for up to 16 hours preserves the US$75 hourly allowance rate. It can cover up to six named sub-accounts, subject to a feasible check schedule. Warn at twelve hours; obtain approval before exceeding sixteen. Sell this only after confirming available delivery capacity. Neither plan promises unlimited changes, continuous monitoring or round-the-clock response.

Review prices after completed projects using actual hours, revisions, contribution and buyer feedback. Three projects or a handful of calls are too small a sample to justify an automatic price-rise rule or a universal close-rate target.

## Direct Indian business offer after validation

Start with the same bounded lead-to-booking journey at **₹25,000 setup plus ₹15,000/month**. Monthly service includes core platform access and up to two total service hours for scheduled checks, a monthly review, support and minor changes. All service time counts toward that allowance. Additional journeys and substantial changes require a separate quote.

One existing eligible channel is included in the setup scope; WhatsApp onboarding or an additional lead source may require separate work. WhatsApp subscriptions, messages, calls, AI, premium actions and third-party tools are separate disclosed charges unless the quote contains a defined allowance. A larger implementation may justify ₹35,000–60,000, but only after its additional work and acceptance criteria have been priced. There is no universal minimum fee that proves a buyer is good or bad.

Offer a **₹4,999 enquiry-process audit**, creditable against setup within fourteen days, only when the buyer wants a diagnosis before a larger decision. Include an owner interview, an authorised review of up to twenty recent enquiry records, one agreed channel test, a one-page findings report and a twenty-minute walkthrough. Budget up to three total work hours. Exclude account repairs, medical or other unnecessary sensitive records and a complete advertising audit. Broader reviews need a separate scope. A short introductory conversation remains free.

Quote customer reactivation separately after checking list provenance, relevant permissions, suppression rules, offer, audience size and message costs. An old contact list is not automatically an eligible campaign audience. Do not promise that a campaign will pay for a year's service.

### Acceptance commitment

Use this commitment in the proposal:

> Before launch, we demonstrate the agreed capture, assignment, follow-up and booking tests. We correct defects against that scope for fourteen calendar days after acceptance at no additional charge.

Distinguish an automated acknowledgement from a useful human response. Provider outages, invalid contact details, consent restrictions and expired account permissions can affect production behaviour. Do not guarantee every enquiry a delivered reply within two minutes or promise indefinite free work. Introduce a measured service level only after agreeing its metric, dependencies, exclusions and remedy with the buyer.

## Selecting an Indian vertical

Run a short discovery exercise after the expansion conditions are met. Interview five owners or authorised managers in each of three accessible verticals. Candidates include home improvement firms, automotive services, skill-training or study-abroad businesses and appointment-based clinics. Start with the segments where you can reach decision-makers and understand the workflow.

Do not submit fabricated patient or customer enquiries as a sales tactic. With the owner's permission, review a small sample of existing enquiries and conduct a labelled test through agreed channels. Measure response during published service hours separately from after-hours handling. A test submission is an observation, not a representative measure of the whole business.

Score the evidence using:

- Relevant enquiry volume and contribution per completed sale.
- A repeated, demonstrable follow-up problem.
- The owner's urgency, budget and willingness to pay for a defined improvement.
- A named staff member responsible for acting on enquiries.
- Fit with the three-workflow build and existing tools.
- Access, data sensitivity, implementation dependencies and the length of the sales cycle.

A high sale price and a slow reply alone do not establish an attractive niche. Use the interviews to select a candidate, then test willingness to pay through a small number of scoped paid engagements. Expand within that vertical after useful results and repeat purchases. If interviews produce no current buying need, reconsider the segment before building a specialist system.

## Prospecting and partner channels

For overseas agency outreach, research a current onboarding need, hiring request or relevant service. Ask who handles implementation and whether capacity is constrained. Show a synthetic-data demonstration and ask about one real project. Do not claim their system is broken from a public website inspection.

For Upwork, apply to jobs matching the defined scope and your skills. Start with a planning allowance of five to ten carefully selected applications per week, subject to relevant job availability and the acquisition budget. Track proposal costs and time. This is an activity experiment, not a prediction that early applications or a fixed daily quota will win.

Help in communities when a question fits your expertise and the community permits it. Agency coaches can become partners only if their students have paying clients, suitable budgets and an actual fulfilment need. Treat those relationships as qualified channels, not guaranteed pipelines.

For Indian discovery, use owner introductions through marketing agencies, web developers and existing business networks. An authorised process review gives you a useful discussion with the owner. Present observed facts and agree what the evidence can establish.

A referral arrangement can be tested at **15% of collected setup service fees and 10% of collected recurring service fees for up to twelve paid service months**. This is a negotiating option, not a default entitlement. Calculate commissions on service fees excluding taxes, provider usage, third-party pass-throughs, discounts and refunds. Pay after customer collection; stop when the customer stops paying, and reconcile any subsequent refund under the written terms. Confirm any professional restrictions before offering fees to advisers or association members.

For example, a ₹25,000 setup produces a ₹3,750 referral fee; a ₹15,000 monthly service produces ₹1,500. Include those costs in the quote's contribution calculation. A referral is not a substitute for qualification, and poor lead quality and poor follow-up can both affect results.

## Pitches grounded in the buyer's situation

### Overseas agency pitch

> I noticed your agency provides [verified service]. I build and test one client's HighLevel lead capture, follow-up and booking journey, then provide the workflow map, test record and handover. Here is a short demo using test data. Do you have a current onboarding or implementation task that needs delivery capacity? The first qualifying sprint is US$450 within the defined scope, with a seven-business-day schedule after access and approvals.

Use the diagnostic when the buyer has an existing issue to investigate. Do not imply that a health check will inevitably find broken systems or invent experience auditing other clients.

### Indian owner pitch

> How do you handle a new enquiry from arrival through booking and payment? If you would like, we can review a small sample together and identify where ownership or follow-up is unclear. I can then quote a defined capture, follow-up and booking setup, train the responsible staff member, and check the agreed process within a monthly allowance. We demonstrate the workflow tests before launch and correct scoped defects for fourteen days.

If the owner wants a deeper diagnosis first, offer the ₹4,999 audit and its exact scope. Do not require an audit when the buyer already has a clear implementation brief.

### Estimate value using contribution

Use recoverable enquiries multiplied by a plausible incremental conversion rate and contribution per sale. Do not treat every neglected enquiry as a lost sale or gross sale value as profit.

For a hypothetical month, 200 enquiries with 10% potentially recoverable gives twenty enquiries. If 20% of those become genuinely additional sales at ₹10,000 contribution each, the incremental contribution is ₹40,000 before agency fees, usage and setup. Those assumptions need evidence; the calculation is not a forecast or proof of causation. Distinguish observed bookings from additional bookings attributable to the change.

## Roadmap and financial scenarios

| Phase | Work | Gate before expanding | If progress is weak |
|---|---|---|---|
| First week | Build the three-workflow demo, test it, record a walkthrough and prepare the scoped proposal | Demo demonstrates stop rules and rescheduling; commercial terms are usable | Simplify the build and fix failed tests before showing it |
| First thirty days | Research sixty suitable agencies, send relevant introductions and submit matched marketplace applications | Target one or two paid pilots; measure the actual funnel and hours | Review targeting, message, proof and urgency at the failing stage |
| Days thirty-one to ninety | Deliver, seek repeat work, quote standard prices and test support where needed | Two accepted paid projects, a repeat purchase, positive contribution and spare capacity | Fix scope, price or delivery before adding a market |
| Indian discovery when ready | Interview five owners in each of three candidate verticals; arrange authorised reviews | Select a segment with a real buying need and secure a scoped paid engagement | Reconsider buyer access, urgency, economics and the offer; do not assume only the job title is wrong |
| Months four to six | Repeat the better-performing work and document tasks suitable for delegation | Consistent demand, affordable contractor hours, documented handover and quality checks | Protect existing service; hire against a defined workload rather than a revenue date |
| Months seven to twelve | Expand the stronger route, diversify referrals and improve renewal | Capacity and contribution support each new commitment | Inspect acquisition, delivery and retention before adding another vertical |

The following are illustrative monthly mixes, not dated targets. They use the existing US$600 eight-hour export support plan, ₹15,000 two-hour Indian direct-business support plan, US$750 sprint and ₹25,000 Indian setup. The exchange rate is an assumed ₹95/USD, not a live quote.

| Monthly mix | Recurring service revenue | One-time project revenue | Total service revenue | Planned delivery hours |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Two export partners and four Indian businesses; one export sprint and one Indian setup | ₹174,000 | ₹96,250 | ₹270,250 | 48 |
| Three export partners and eight Indian businesses; two export sprints and one Indian setup | ₹291,000 | ₹167,500 | ₹458,500 | 76 |
| Four export partners and sixteen Indian businesses; two export sprints and two Indian setups | ₹468,000 | ₹192,500 | ₹660,500 | 112 |

Delivery hours assume every support allowance is used and each project takes twelve hours. Actual Indian setup time must be validated; these scenarios apply to the same bounded core journey. Larger setups need their own hours. Add acquisition, administration, defect correction and contingency before deciding whether a month fits your capacity.

In the first mix, valuing all 48 delivery hours at an assumed ₹1,500/hour costs ₹72,000. Deduct an assumed Unlimited platform cost of ₹28,215 and ₹5,000 other overhead from total service revenue, leaving ₹165,035 before acquisition and administration labour, payment fees, commissions, tax, defect correction and unbudgeted costs. This is an economic contribution illustration, not take-home income. Separately reimbursed usage is excluded from revenue and cost. Actual plan choice depends on hosted account capacity and paid features.

A ₹6 lakh-plus revenue month can therefore require both a retained client base and additional project sales. Twenty Indian retainers at ₹15,000 alone produce ₹3 lakh; thirty at ₹25,000 produce ₹7.5 lakh. Do not present ₹6–10 lakh as an automatic result of twenty to thirty clients.

## India and export administration

Confirm GST registration requirements and export-of-services eligibility with your accountant for the actual engagement. For a registered person using the eligible route to export without payment of integrated tax, the GST portal describes filing an LUT before the qualifying supply. The LUT applies to the relevant financial year. It is not a blanket requirement for every person issuing a foreign invoice. [Official GST LUT guidance](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/refund/Furnishing_of_Letter_of_Undertaking_for_Export_of_Goods_or_Services.htm).

Before choosing a payment provider, confirm availability for your business, accepted currencies, total fees, settlement route and the remittance documentation it supplies for that route. Retain invoices and reconcile each payment. Have your accountant and bank confirm which records are appropriate; do not assume FIRA, FIRC and other certificates are interchangeable in every situation.

Use a written scope, payment milestones, revision allowance, access and data responsibilities, ownership and termination arrangements. Collect recurring service fees in advance. Check actual channel eligibility and provider requirements before quoting messaging or calling. Use [Delivery and repeat business](04-delivery-and-repeat-business.md) for the current India phone limitations and consent requirements.

Work in partner-owned accounts for agency delivery. Use Starter for your own demonstration while its three-sub-account capacity fits; upgrade before hosting more accounts than the plan allows. Two ₹15,000 customers may cover much of a US$297 platform invoice at the assumed exchange rate, but that alone does not cover their labour, acquisition and other service costs. See [Offers pricing and economics](02-offers-pricing-and-economics.md) for the dated platform references and costing examples.

## Weekly operating discipline

Protect sixty to ninety minutes on working days for research, relevant outreach and follow-up within the acquisition budget. Urgent client incidents and committed delivery take priority; reschedule the acquisition block rather than abandoning it. Do not promise overnight delivery or uninterrupted availability across several time zones. Agree a working window you can sustain.

Review qualified first contacts, useful replies, calls held, proposals decided, paid projects, accepted projects, repeat purchases, actual hours, collected revenue and contribution each week. Keep marketplace application costs separate. Use counts alongside percentages while the sample is small.

The next actions are to build the minimum demo, prepare the scoped offer, research the first 25 agencies, send fifteen relevant introductions and submit only matched marketplace applications. Sell and finish one useful paid project, then earn the next purchase before expanding the service.
