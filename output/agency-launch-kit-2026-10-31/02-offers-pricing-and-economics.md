# HighLevel Offers Pricing and Economics

Launch with one project and one optional recurring service. Prices below are starting hypotheses to validate through paid sales. All dollar fees are US dollars; rupee fees are separate India-market quotes, not currency conversions. Fees exclude applicable tax and payment processing charges.

## The minimum starting offer

**HighLevel Lead to Booking Sprint**

> I will configure and test one client's lead capture, follow-up and booking journey inside your HighLevel account, then hand over the workflow map, test results and a walkthrough.

| Component | Included scope |
|---|---|
| Client environment | One existing sub-account, one business, one location and one language |
| Lead capture | One native HighLevel form or one already functioning native lead source |
| Sales tracking | One pipeline with up to six stages and assignment to one agreed owner or team |
| Booking | One calendar with agreed hours, buffers and cancellation or reschedule rules |
| Workflow one | Capture, deduplicate where supported, create or update opportunity, assign and notify |
| Workflow two | Up to three follow-up messages with consent checks and reply, booking and opt-out stop rules |
| Workflow three | Booking confirmation and up to two reminders, respecting cancellation and rescheduling |
| Channel | One agreed eligible outbound channel; email is the default demo channel |
| Copy | Adapt client-supplied copy for up to six distinct outbound messages across the workflows |
| Reporting | A baseline record and a simple view of new leads, bookings and overdue actions using available fields |
| Handover | Workflow map, test record, one recorded walkthrough and one 30-minute handover call |
| Revisions | Two consolidated revision rounds within the agreed process |
| Defect correction | Fourteen calendar days after acceptance for defects against agreed scope |

The total outbound copy allowance is six unique messages. Reusing the same approved confirmation in another location does not add a new message; extra copy or journey branches require a scope change. The workflows may send more than six messages in total as different contacts pass through them.

Aim to complete the sprint in seven business days after payment, access, approved content and necessary channel approvals are available. Pause the delivery clock when a dependency is missing, and tell the buyer promptly. Plan up to 12 delivery hours, including testing and handover; this is an internal costing allowance, while the buyer purchases the defined result.

Advanced routing, multiple funnels, landing-page design, custom code, data migration, AI agents, telephony, new ad campaigns and additional integrations are separately quoted. Existing workflow repair can be sold as an alternative sprint after diagnosis, rather than silently added to a new build.

## Starting prices

| Buyer | First two qualifying pilot projects | Standard sprint | Optional monthly support |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Overseas agency | US$450 each | US$750 | US$600 for up to eight delivery hours |
| Indian agency | ₹15,000 each | ₹25,000 | ₹15,000 for up to eight delivery hours |

The two pilot places are a total launch allowance across markets. Track them explicitly. Standard pricing applies after they are filled. If a pilot contains exceptional work, rescope it rather than using the low price for a larger project.

For direct overseas sales, quote in one agreed invoice currency. For the initial price test use USD; if a UK buyer requires GBP, issue a GBP quote with a dated conversion and a short validity period. Do not leave currency conversion open until payment.

**Payment terms:** 50% before work starts and 50% after the acceptance demonstration, before production activation or final handover. Platform marketplaces use their permitted milestone and payment processes. Collect recurring fees monthly in advance. State cancellation, refund, confidentiality and account-access terms in the actual agreement.

If the price is challenged, reduce the process or the delivery allowance. A smaller paid repair can be US$200 or ₹7,500 for one diagnosed issue and up to three hours including testing and notes. Do not sell an unknown integration failure at that price without first defining the fault and acceptance criteria.

## Optional workflow diagnostic

A US$149 diagnostic is available for an overseas agency with a specific existing concern. It covers one journey containing up to three named workflows and a short findings recording, within two total work hours including access review, inspection and reporting. It excludes repairs, a full account audit and a deliverability investigation. Quote broader investigations separately. The diagnostic is not a compulsory first purchase, and it may conclude that no further paid work is necessary. A repair is a separate purchase with its own acceptance criteria.

## What monthly support actually buys

The US$600 or ₹15,000 plan covers up to eight delivery hours per calendar month across up to three named sub-accounts. It includes scheduled weekly checks of the agreed workflows, minor fixes, agreed small changes, a short weekly update and one monthly review. All checks, calls, fixes, changes and documentation consume the allowance.

There is one active work request at a time. Acknowledge ordinary requests within one business day in the agreed service window. Estimate the completion time after diagnosis. Monitoring is scheduled, not continuous; acknowledgement is not a resolution guarantee.

Unused hours expire at month-end because the fee reserves capacity. Send a warning at six hours used. Stop additional chargeable work at eight hours unless the buyer approves more. Approved extra work is US$75/hour or ₹2,000/hour, in half-hour increments, or a new fixed project. New client launches use the sprint price, rather than being added automatically to the support queue.

Offer support only where continuing work exists. If the buyer needs a build every few months and no ongoing checks, stay available on a project basis and agree a review date.

After proving demand and confirming capacity, an overseas buyer can instead reserve up to sixteen hours across up to six named sub-accounts for US$1,200/month. All service time counts, there is one active request at a time, and new client launches remain separate projects. Warn at twelve hours and require approval before exceeding sixteen. This preserves the US$75 hourly allowance rate. The service window, scheduled rather than continuous checks, monthly expiry and approved US$75/hour overage terms are the same as the eight-hour plan.

## Direct Indian business package after validation

For a suitable business that wants you to host its HighLevel sub-account, start by testing **₹25,000 setup plus ₹15,000/month**, for one location and the same core journey. Monthly service includes core platform access and up to two total service hours for scheduled checks, one monthly review, support and minor changes. New journeys and marketing campaign management are extra.

WhatsApp, AI, messages, calls, premium workflow actions, domains, external integrations and third-party subscriptions are billed separately at disclosed provider cost unless the written quote includes a defined allowance. This direct-business package is an optional later route; do not start selling it alongside the agency pilot without delivery capacity.

Use the direct-business expansion conditions in [Operator strategy](06-operator-strategy.md): two accepted paid projects, a repeat purchase or renewal, positive delivery contribution and six spare hours a week for a two-week discovery exercise. The base setup includes one existing eligible channel. New WhatsApp onboarding, additional lead sources or larger journeys may require a separate quote; ₹35,000–60,000 can be tested for a larger implementation only after costing its actual scope. It is not the price of the unchanged minimum sprint.

For an Indian owner who needs diagnosis first, offer a ₹4,999 enquiry-process audit, credited toward setup if purchased within fourteen days. Include an owner interview, authorised review of up to twenty recent enquiry records, one agreed channel test, a one-page report and a twenty-minute walkthrough within three total work hours. Exclude repairs, unnecessary sensitive records and a complete advertising audit. A short introductory call is free; the audit is optional when the implementation brief is already clear.

The acceptance commitment is to demonstrate agreed functional tests before launch and correct scoped defects for fourteen calendar days after acceptance. Do not guarantee that every enquiry receives a delivered reply within two minutes, or offer indefinite free work. Automated acknowledgement, human response and provider delivery are different measures.

The customer should be able to explain how the fee could pay for itself. For example, ₹15,000 service plus an assumed ₹2,000 usage budget requires two genuinely additional sales at ₹10,000 contribution per sale to exceed monthly cost. In the first month, adding ₹25,000 setup takes the cost to ₹42,000, requiring five such sales to exceed it. These are break-even illustrations, not expected results. Include the client's sales cycle and ability to serve those customers.

For potential upside, use recoverable enquiries × incremental conversion rate × contribution per sale. Twenty potentially recoverable enquiries at an assumed 20% conversion and ₹10,000 contribution represent ₹40,000 before service fees, usage and setup. Validate those assumptions; twenty neglected enquiries do not equal twenty lost sales.

## Referral cost and pricing presentation

If commercially justified, test a referral agreement at 15% of collected setup service fees and 10% of collected recurring service fees for up to twelve paid service months. Calculate it after discounts and exclude tax, provider usage, third-party pass-throughs and refunds. Pay after collection, stop when the customer stops paying, and reconcile later refunds under the agreement. Check any professional restrictions before offering fees to advisers.

At ₹25,000 setup and ₹15,000 monthly service, those referral costs are ₹3,750 and ₹1,500 respectively. Deduct them when testing contribution; they are not included in the uncommissioned examples below. Do not stack referral payments from multiple sources without pricing their total cost.

Use [Client offers and starting prices](07-client-offers-and-starting-prices.md) as the concise sales reference. The broad task catalogue requires recosting before being used for current quotes. Keep its service inventory for estimation, and reprice from effort, revisions, acquisition, platform allocation and support responsibility rather than multiplying every price by an arbitrary factor.

## Platform costs and plan choice

HighLevel's public pricing checked on 31 October 2026 lists Starter at US$97/month with three sub-accounts, Unlimited at US$297/month with unlimited sub-accounts, and Founder at US$497/month. Some help and FAQ text still uses the older Pro naming. [HighLevel pricing](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing).

For agency implementation, work in the partner's account. Your own subscription is for demonstration or internal operations; it is not a mandatory software charge for every partner engagement. Use Starter while its account capacity is sufficient. If you use one sub-account internally, only two remain within its three-account limit. Upgrade before exceeding capacity and price the growth step into the business.

At a deliberately assumed ₹95/USD planning rate, these monthly plan costs are ₹9,215, ₹28,215 and ₹47,215. Use the actual invoice exchange rate, tax and card charges for real costing. These are not live currency quotations.

HighLevel lists WhatsApp at US$10/month per enabled sub-account plus usage. Its current billing guide describes per-message charges and allowances effective 1 October 2026. Do not promise unlimited free customer conversations based on older comparison pages. AI subscriptions and usage can also be additional. [HighLevel billing guide](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001156-highlevel-pricing-guide), [WhatsApp billing guide](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001428-whatsapp-pricing-billing-and-rebilling-guide).

Enable paid services only after the account payer understands the charge. At launch, avoid an expensive plan upgrade solely for branding or automatic rebilling.

## Cost your time and protect contribution

Use a realistic replacement cost for delivery time, even while you do the work yourself. Also track unpaid sales, administration and revisions. Cash left in the bank is not the same as profit after the owner's labour.

Illustrative overseas standard sprint, using the assumed ₹95/USD rate:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| US$750 revenue | ₹71,250 |
| Twelve delivery hours at assumed ₹1,500/hour | ₹18,000 |
| Assumed allocation for tools and administration | ₹2,500 |
| Contribution before acquisition, payment fees, tax and other overhead | ₹50,750 |

At the US$450 pilot price, the same assumptions leave ₹22,250. If the work expands to 25 hours, most of that pilot contribution disappears. Recording hours is essential to finding the sustainable standard scope.

For a local hosted model, eight clients at ₹15,000 produce ₹120,000/month. Deducting an assumed Unlimited subscription at ₹28,215, 16 monthly delivery hours at ₹750/hour and ₹5,000 other overhead leaves ₹74,785 before sales and administration time, payment fees, taxes and unbudgeted costs. Client-funded usage is excluded from both revenue and cost in this illustration. At only two or three clients, platform allocation is materially higher per client.

For individual projects, estimate labour, tool allocation and delivery risk first. A pricing floor can be calculated as estimated cost divided by one minus the required contribution margin. Then test whether the buyer values the result at that price. A mathematically profitable quote still needs a willing buyer.

## Proposal template

> **Project:** Lead to Booking Sprint for [agency] and [client].
>
> **Current problem:** [Buyer-confirmed delivery problem and business consequence].
>
> **Outcome:** One functioning lead capture, follow-up and booking journey, demonstrated against the acceptance checklist.
>
> **Included:** [Source], [pipeline], [calendar], three workflows, six-message copy allowance, test record, walkthrough and two revision rounds.
>
> **Dependencies:** Named approver, least-privilege account access, supplied copy, consent requirements and active eligible channel.
>
> **Schedule:** Seven business days after dependencies and deposit are complete.
>
> **Fee:** [Currency and amount], 50% deposit and 50% after acceptance demonstration before activation or final handover. Software and usage are paid by the agency.
>
> **Acceptance:** All agreed functional test cases pass. Buyer supplies one consolidated feedback list within three business days of demonstration. Silence is not treated as approval; delay shifts the schedule.
>
> **After launch:** Fourteen days of scoped defect correction. Optional eight-hour monthly support at [price]. Defect correction does not consume paid support hours.
>
> **Additional work:** Written scope and price approval before work starts.
>
> **Quote validity:** Fourteen calendar days. Production activation requires the buyer's explicit approval.
