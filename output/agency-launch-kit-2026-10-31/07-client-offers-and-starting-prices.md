# HighLevel Client Offers and Starting Prices

For agencies that need implementation capacity, we configure and test a defined client lead-to-booking journey inside your HighLevel account. For an Indian business, we can scope the same journey around your team's enquiry process, with core platform access and a defined monthly service allowance.

All prices are before applicable tax and payment-provider charges. US dollar prices and Indian rupee prices are separate market quotes. Your written proposal confirms the included scope, schedule and dependencies.

## Agency implementation

The **Lead to Booking Sprint** covers one existing client sub-account, one business, one location, one language and one eligible outbound channel.

- One native form or already functioning native lead source.
- One sales pipeline with up to six stages and an agreed assignment process.
- One booking calendar with hours, buffers and rescheduling rules.
- Three workflows for capture and assignment, follow-up, and booking confirmation and reminders.
- Up to six distinct outbound messages adapted from supplied copy.
- Functional testing, a workflow map, a recorded walkthrough, a thirty-minute handover call and two revision rounds.

Larger overseas support arrangements can be quoted where capacity permits.

For an agency with a specific existing concern, an optional US$149 diagnostic covers one journey with up to three named workflows and a short findings recording within two total work hours. Repairs, full account audits and deliverability investigations are extra.

## Indian business enquiry and booking package

**₹25,000 setup plus ₹15,000/month** for one location, one language and the same core journey: one existing eligible lead source, one pipeline, one calendar and three workflows. Your team receives the workflow handover and a clear process for responding to enquiries.

The monthly fee includes core platform access and up to two total service hours for scheduled checks, a monthly review, support and minor changes. Additional sources, new channel onboarding, larger journeys and substantial changes need a separate quote.

It excludes repairs, unnecessary sensitive records and a complete advertising audit. The audit fee is credited toward setup if purchased within fourteen days. A short introductory conversation is free.

## Schedule and acceptance

The sprint aims for seven business days after the deposit, required access, approved content and necessary channel approvals are complete. Missing dependencies can move the schedule. The proposal identifies the approver and acceptance tests before work starts.

We demonstrate the agreed capture, assignment, follow-up and booking tests before launch. We correct defects against that scope for fourteen calendar days after acceptance at no additional charge. This correction period does not consume a paid support allowance. Bookings and sales also depend on the offer, lead quality and the team's follow-up; they are not guaranteed.

Project payment is 50% before work starts and 50% after the acceptance demonstration, before activation or final handover. Monthly service is paid in advance. Marketplace engagements follow the platform's permitted payment process.
