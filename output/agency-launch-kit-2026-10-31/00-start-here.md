# HighLevel Agency Launch Plan From India

Prepared for an automation specialist based in Lucknow. Planning date: 31 October 2026.

Start as an implementation partner for small overseas agencies already using HighLevel. Sell one repeatable client onboarding project, deliver it inside the agency's account, and earn repeat work through dependable delivery and a defined support plan. Use Indian marketing agencies as a second prospecting channel after the overseas offer has had a fair test.

This is the recommended starting bet for your skills, not a measured claim about which business model has the highest success rate. Validate it with paid projects before expanding. A flexible budget and schedule let you run the experiment properly; they do not replace proof that a buyer will pay.

## The starting decisions

| Decision | Starting choice |
|---|---|
| Business | HighLevel implementation and automation studio |
| First buyer | Owner or delivery lead at a small UK agency already serving paying clients through HighLevel |
| Buyer problem | New client onboarding work is delayed or keeps pulling the owner into technical delivery |
| Initial offer | HighLevel Lead to Booking Sprint |
| Minimum delivery | One client sub-account, one native lead source, one pipeline, one calendar and three workflows |
| Pilot price | US$450 for an overseas project within the two-project launch allowance |
| Standard project price | US$750 once the two pilot places are filled |
| Recurring offer | US$600/month for up to eight delivery hours across up to three approved sub-accounts |
| Software responsibility | Partner agency pays for its HighLevel account and all client usage |
| Primary acquisition | Research relevant agencies, contact owners personally, show a working demo, sell one paid pilot |
| First milestone | One paid project accepted by the buyer, followed by a second project or a support renewal |

All agency fees in this kit are proposed starting prices, before applicable tax and payment-provider charges. They are not market averages. Dollar amounts mean US dollars. The pricing document defines scope and extra charges.

## Your first seven days

1. Build one demonstrator for a fictional home service business using synthetic contacts and eligible test channels.
2. Record a three-minute walkthrough showing the lead arriving, assignment, follow-up stopping after a reply, and booking reminders changing after a reschedule.
3. Prepare a one-page offer using the scope and terms in the pricing document.
4. Research 25 UK agencies, retaining only those with a visible HighLevel connection and an existing client service.
5. Send 15 individually researched introductions through appropriate business channels. Ask about current onboarding capacity, rather than assuming a fault exists.
6. Apply to a few clearly matched marketplace jobs with the same demonstration.
7. Review actual replies and calls. A polished demo matters only if it helps start a relevant commercial conversation.

Keep the next objective small: sell and finish one useful project. Start measuring repeat purchase, delivery hours and contribution as soon as that project is complete.
