## Read this first

**What the charge column includes.** The service charge covers only the stated delivery labour and its markup. Add the separately listed platform allocation, paid modules, PAYG, vendors and selected support. It is not an all-in package price.

**How ranges work.** Low = fewer estimated hours at the low labour rate and low markup; high = more hours at the high rate and high markup. Choose the actual scope/rate first. Rupee markup = cost × markup percentage; charge = cost + markup.

**HighLevel cost vs actual bill.** Your plan, account invoices and usage were not supplied. H/U rates are published planning inputs, not a claim about your actual spend. Choose your existing plan, allocate its cost once per paying location, and check live tariffs before quoting.

**Simple quote structure.** One-time: delivery charge + actual one-time vendor purchases. Monthly: allocated platform + selected add-ons + billable usage + vendor subscriptions + selected support. Apply each line’s own markup; count shared/included work once.

**Select cost layers, not every option.** Message channels and paid tools shown in a row are alternatives or optional dependencies unless the scope requires them. Email-only reminders do not require WhatsApp or phone rental. Reuse subscriptions already paid; avoid stacking optional AI plans on PAYG unnecessarily.

**Markup policy.** The India-focused bands are suggested pricing guidance, not measured market averages. Software and usage default to at cost; optional 0–10% administration markup is shown. Client ad spend, rewards and gateway fees pass through at cost. The customer catalogue uses the same service-charge ranges and default at-cost recovery for software/usage; any optional administration markup requires a stated custom quote.
