## Understand your price

**One-time setup.** The work to configure, build and test the agreed service. One brand, one language and one location unless the offer says otherwise.

**Recurring work.** Optional care, campaign management or content production. Monthly production services show their monthly fee and any initial setup.

**Software and usage.** Access subscriptions, paid tools, messages, AI activity, calls and payment-provider charges, shown separately in the proposal.

Choose only the work you need. When several services share the same setup, we scope them together and charge shared work once.
