# Business Services Catalogue

**Services & indicative fee ranges | India | 31 October 2026**

Practical services to attract enquiries, organise follow-up, take bookings and payments, improve customer communication and simplify everyday operations. Choose one service or combine several into a connected solution.

The ranges below are service fees for the stated scope, in INR before applicable tax. Your quote confirms the exact fee. Software access, paid tools, message/call/AI usage, external purchases and optional support are separate unless explicitly included. The lower end assumes straightforward delivery using supplied material; more work within the scope moves the fee towards the upper end.

## How your quote is built

**One-time or recurring.** Each row states whether the fee is one-time, per campaign, per session or per month. Monthly production fees include the stated deliverables; initial setup is shown separately.

**Software and optional tools.** We confirm the access needed and identify subscriptions you already pay for. A separate paid tool is added only when the selected solution needs it.

**Usage and third parties.** Messages, calls, AI activity, payment processing and external suppliers are billed separately at actual provider cost in this guide. Any different handling or service charge must be stated in your written quote.

**Support after launch.** Optional support covers an agreed work allowance. Production or campaign management already included in a service is not charged again as support.

**Shared setup.** When services share the same setup, we scope the combined solution and count shared work once. Unless stated otherwise, one brand, one language and one location are included.

**Your written quote.** Shows the selected service and quantities, exact setup fee, recurring service work, software, estimated usage, external purchases, support allowance, billing terms and applicable taxes.
